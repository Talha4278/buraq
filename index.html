Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with BuraqDev.

Do you build custom client software or only your own SaaS products? +

Both. We operate as a hybrid venture studio and custom engineering lab. We build, scale, and maintain our own proprietary SaaS platforms (like ClinSyst and WABook) while simultaneously partnering with funded startups and established businesses to engineer their custom web and mobile applications. Because we manage live software with real paying users daily, our custom client codebases benefit from tested, real-world multi-tenant architectures and operational discipline.

What is your pricing and milestone structure? +

We work exclusively with fixed-milestone pricing. Before writing a line of code, you receive an itemized technical scope, architecture blueprint, and sprint schedule. You review and approve deliverables at each milestone before releasing payments.

How long does a typical MVP or web build take? +

Standard production-ready MVPs ship in 6 to 8 weeks. Larger enterprise platforms or complex integrations typically require 8 to 12 weeks. We determine an exact, contractually backed timeline during our discovery sprint.

Do I own 100% of the code and intellectual property? +

Yes, entirely. Upon milestone payment, 100% intellectual property ownership of your custom repository, design assets, and architecture is transferred directly to your organization. Zero licensing fees, no proprietary lock-in.

What technologies and frameworks do you use? +

We build with modern, industry-standard technologies: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, React Native, PostgreSQL, Supabase, TailwindCSS, AWS, and Cloudflare. This ensures your in-house team can easily scale the codebase in the future.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support? +

Every custom build includes 30 days of comprehensive post-launch warranty to guarantee stability. We also offer monthly support retainers for continuous feature iteration, security updates, and infrastructure monitoring.
